# LIFEPATH: EVERY CHOICE COUNTS
## Ultimate Original Life-Simulation Game Development Prompt



########################################################################
MASTER PROMPT: LIFEPATH — ORIGINAL LIFE SIMULATOR
########################################################################
Create a complete, playable, mobile-first life simulation game called LIFEPATH: Every Choice Counts. It may be inspired by the broad life-simulator genre and the kinds of systems players enjoy in BitLife, but it must use original writing, original artwork, original iconography, original screen composition, original code, and a distinct brand identity. Do not extract or imitate proprietary assets, exact screenshots, logos, or undocumented private implementation details. Build an actual functioning game, not a static mockup. Every visible button must work, every screen must have meaningful content, and every action must validate, change state where appropriate, and provide feedback.

The player begins a fictional life, makes choices, ages through life stages, develops relationships, studies, works, earns and spends money, explores activities, encounters unexpected events, and eventually reaches an end-of-life summary. Make the experience replayable through varied starting circumstances, event chains, career paths, relationships, achievements, and optional legacy continuation. Use data-driven systems, deterministic seeded randomness, robust persistence, accessible UI, and honest error handling. If any detail is unspecified, choose a consistent solution and document it. Never claim a feature, test, asset, save, or deployment exists unless it actually does.



########################################################################
PRODUCT PILLARS AND PLAYER EXPERIENCE
########################################################################
The game must be choice-driven rather than a sequence of meaningless taps. Decisions should influence immediate and long-term outcomes. Consequences should be understandable, but not every outcome must be predictable. Keep randomness bounded and context-sensitive. The player should be able to recover from setbacks through alternate opportunities, support systems, retraining, rest, lower-cost choices, and new goals.

Build a clear mobile experience with a readable year feed, persistent navigation, concise event cards, large touch targets, and a prominent Age Up action. Minimize unnecessary modal interruptions. Support portrait phones first, then tablets and desktop. Preserve progress through autosave and recover gracefully from interruption. Provide content filters, reduced motion, scalable text, high contrast, screen-reader support, and keyboard navigation.

Use fictional people, employers, institutions, brands, and locations. Represent difficult events respectfully and non-graphically. Avoid actionable real-world criminal instructions, explicit sexual content, graphic harm, stereotypes, or defamatory depictions of real people. Core gameplay must function offline.



########################################################################
VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM
########################################################################
Create a distinct editorial-playful visual identity. Define reusable design tokens for colors, typography, spacing, radius, elevation, borders, focus rings, icon sizes, and motion timing. Use a warm light theme and a deep dark theme, with a controlled accent color and semantic success, warning, danger, and neutral colors. Never rely on color alone to communicate status.

Typography tokens: Display, H1, H2, H3, body, small body, caption, button, and numeric stat. Body text must remain readable at increased text sizes. Cards use consistent padding, hierarchy, and spacing. Buttons require default, pressed, focused, disabled, loading, success, and error states as applicable. Touch targets should be at least 44×44 CSS pixels.

Create original scalable icons with consistent stroke/fill rules. Character portraits may use original vector avatars, generated original art, or simple stylized silhouettes. Do not use BitLife assets or screenshots. Animations should be short and purposeful: subtle screen transitions, modal fade/scale, stat count-up, event reveal, and restrained achievement celebration. Reduced-motion mode must replace nonessential movement with immediate or opacity-only changes.

Responsive rules: narrow phone uses a single-column stack and bottom navigation; wider layouts may center content with a readable max width and optional side summary. Avoid clipping, horizontal overflow, and overlapping primary actions. Maintain the same information architecture at every breakpoint.



########################################################################
APP SHELL AND NAVIGATION
########################################################################
The app shell contains a contextual header, main viewport, persistent primary navigation, and a global overlay layer. Header content includes character name, age, current location, and a compact life-stage/status cue. Do not crowd the header with every stat.

Primary tabs:
1. Life — current-year feed, event history, year summary, and Age Up.
2. Activities — categorized actions and opportunities.
3. Relationships — family, friends, partners, dependents, rivals, and social network.
4. Assets — cash, recurring income/expenses, debt, property, vehicles, valuables, and business holdings.
5. Profile — identity, attributes, education, career history, health overview, achievements, and timeline.

Secondary screens: Education, Career, Health, Romance, Family, Crime & Justice, Fame, Travel, Housing, Vehicles, Business, Pets, Will & Estate, Achievements, Settings, Help, and Legacy. Use consistent back navigation and preserve scroll/selection state where practical. Only one blocking modal may be active. Queue noncritical notifications and group repetitive updates.

Life is the default landing screen. It contains an identity strip, “This Year” card, chronological narrative entries, suggested actions, and a prominent Age Up button. Age Up is disabled while a mandatory choice remains unresolved. Never hide a critical decision behind an unrelated screen.

########################################################################
LIFE STAGES AND TRANSITIONS
########################################################################
Define life stages as configurable data with age range, available actions, event pools, responsibilities, UI guidance, and transition hooks. Childhood focuses on home, school, hobbies, friendships, and age-appropriate agency. Teen years expand education, clubs, eligible part-time work, identity expression, friendships, and age-appropriate romance. Young adulthood unlocks independent housing, advanced education, full-time work, travel, and deeper financial responsibility. Adulthood supports broad careers, family systems, property, business, caregiving, and long-term goals. Later adulthood emphasizes retirement, mentoring, health maintenance, family milestones, estate planning, and reflection. End-of-life offers a respectful summary and valid continuation options.

Age boundaries must be consistent and configurable. Never show adult-only systems to children. Stage transitions should be announced once, unlock relevant activities, and create a history entry. Do not assume that every player follows the same education, relationship, career, or family path.



########################################################################
SAVE DATA AND RECOVERY
########################################################################
Use a versioned schema and migration functions. Store character identity, age/date, location, life stage, stats, family graph, relationships, education, career, finances, assets, health state, event history, pending events, achievements, settings, and legacy metadata. Use stable IDs and explicit references. Avoid storing derived values redundantly unless needed; verify cached values after load.

Autosave after character creation, each completed year, major transactions, relationship-changing decisions, and settings changes. Use atomic writes and a recoverable prior snapshot when feasible. A failed save must never be reported as successful. Save slots show character name, age, stage, location, and last-saved time. Include empty, active, incompatible, recoverable, and corrupted states. Never overwrite corrupt source data silently.

Test app interruption during age-up, duplicate taps, device clock changes, low storage, interrupted writes, malformed names, missing relationship references, large histories, invalid legacy data, and migration failure. Preserve the last valid save and explain recovery options clearly.



########################################################################
ACCESSIBILITY, LOCALIZATION, AUDIO, AND MOTION
########################################################################
Support scalable text, screen-reader semantics, visible focus, keyboard navigation, high contrast, reduced motion, non-color status cues, and mobile touch targets. Every icon-only control requires an accessible label. Modals trap focus and return it to the opener. Toasts announce politely and never interrupt critical decisions.

Externalize all strings with pluralization and interpolation support. Format currency, dates, and numbers by locale. Support Unicode names and long translated strings. Avoid concatenating fragments that create broken grammar. Content filters must apply to generated events, history previews, and notifications; filtered content must not leak through labels.

Audio is optional. Provide original tap, confirm, notification, achievement, and age-up cues, with independent music/effects volume and mute. Haptics are configurable. Motion guidance: screen transition 120–180 ms; modal 140–200 ms; button feedback under 100 ms; stat transition 250–450 ms; event reveal subtle; achievement restrained and skippable. Pause animation when backgrounded. Reduced-motion mode removes nonessential movement.



########################################################################
BALANCE, PRIVACY, AND PLAYER TRUST
########################################################################
Balance wages, costs, asset values, education, care, travel, gifts, and business operations through configurable tables. Avoid runaway compounding. Provide multiple recovery paths after setbacks. Prevent duplicate rewards, negative costs, invalid quantities, overflow, time rollback, duplicated inheritance, and transaction replay. Validate on the simulation layer, not only in UI.

Core gameplay is offline. Any analytics must be disclosed, privacy-conscious, aggregate, and opt-out capable. Do not collect real names, contact lists, precise location, or sensitive user-entered content for gameplay analytics. Monetization, if any, must be transparent and must not block core progression or pressure players during vulnerable events. The default design assumes no pay-to-win requirement.
